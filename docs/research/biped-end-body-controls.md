# Direct end and body controls

The prepared rig overlay now adds tapered hand/foot end cages, a pelvis cage and a thin master outline, using existing controller IDs. Their sizing/direction comes from mapped bone pivots and direct bone children, without requiring names or inspecting Skin vertices. An endpoint selects the Goal while its effective contact/base IK weight is positive; at zero it selects FK End. A mapping that shares pelvis with a limb start keeps the existing directly manipulable shape for that one controller. The master is picked on its outline, excluding its broad interior so it does not intercept feet/body selections. Existing visibility, frozen-state, region picking and on-top paths are reused; no main-UI panel, metadata migration or source mesh change is added.

Four older tests needed the new expected proxy counts; their source-preservation and storage-reuse assertions remain. A further regression reproduced master disappearance when limb helper sizes were zero.
